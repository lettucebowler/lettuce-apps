## Year-to-date

Since the start of the year I have decided to de-emphasize the number of books I read and focus instead on enjoying my reading without any pressure on the number of books. It's a flawed metric to begin with because different books have different lengths and complexities that make it impossible to compare the totals between two people. I am choosing to continue to track because it can be cool to look back at what I have read and see all the covers together in the grid. So far this year I have completed 33 books! I've watched 48 movies so far, with 13 of them being rewatches.
